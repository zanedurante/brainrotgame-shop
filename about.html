Find something to play.

Game Slop Now brings together free games you can open in a browser, from quick solo rounds to campaigns and shared rooms with friends.

Choose your kind of game

The collection spans arcade games, card battles, action games and strategy. Each game card gives you a short description, the supported player count and the controls to expect. Choose Play to open the game directly.

  • For a quick solo session: look for a one-player game, such as Wowzaworka, where you collect wowzas, dodge grumps and chain pickups for a blast.
  • For a longer campaign: MDCCCII starts on a world map in May 1754, with exploration, colonies, trade, diplomacy, armies and fleets. You can play solo or share a campaign with up to 16 players.
  • For playing together: check the player count on the card, then use that game's multiplayer menu. Room creation, joining and saving depend on the game.

The games run in the browser without a separate download. Start with the instructions and controls inside each game. Keyboard, mouse and touch support vary; the cards identify the supported inputs.

What the rankings mean

Sort by number of plays orders games by the shared total on their Play buttons. These totals count launches from this portal, rather than unique people, completed matches or visits made directly to a game. Lifetime totals include the collection's starting values and subsequent recorded clicks.

Trending uses recorded Play clicks from the last seven days. Sort by newest update uses detected changes in the deployed games. A game can move in these lists while the page is open.

One collection, two addresses

Game Slop Now is also available at brainrotgame.shop. Both portal addresses show the same collection and share the same play counts. Games open at the addresses shown on their Play buttons and may have their own storage and multiplayer features.
